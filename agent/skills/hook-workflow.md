Use when the user asks for hooks, hook ideas, hook angles, or how to start a piece of content.

## When to Trigger

- "give me hook ideas" / "write hooks for this"
- "what hooks would work" / "generate hook angles"
- "how do I start this" / "what's a good opening"
- "make it hook" / "improve the hook"
- After content analysis identifies the subject and hook

## Hook Types to Generate

For each request, generate 3–5 distinct hook angles from the same source material.

### Pattern 1: The Contrast Hook
Show two opposing states, before/after, or expected vs. unexpected.
> "I ate the same meal every day for 30 days. Here's what happened to my energy."

### Pattern 2: The Statistic Hook
Lead with a surprising or counter-intuitive number.
> "93% of people wash their hands wrong. Here's the right way."

### Pattern 3: The POV Hook
Drop the viewer into someone else's perspective immediately.
> "POV: You're a first-generation college student and your parents don't understand."

### Pattern 4: The Object Close-Up Hook
Start on a mysterious or emotionally charged object.
> "Close-up on the ring he never returned."

### Pattern 5: The Bold Claim Hook
Make a declarative statement that challenges a common belief.
> "You don't need willpower. You need systems."

### Pattern 6: The Loop Hook
Start mid-action so the viewer needs to see the beginning.
> "[Beat drops] She wasn't supposed to be in the club that night."

### Pattern 7: The Question Hook
Ask something the viewer immediately wants answered.
> "What's the one thing successful people do that others don't?"

### Pattern 8: The Pattern Interrupt Hook
Something that breaks the expected format of the content.
> "This recipe has been in my family for 6 generations. I almost didn't share it."

## First-Second Retention Rules

- The hook must deliver value in the first 0.5 seconds (visual) and first 3 seconds (verbal)
- On mobile (autoplay, muted): visual hook is mandatory
- Text overlays on the first frame dramatically increase pause rate
- Faces looking at camera in the first frame = higher retention
- Starting mid-sentence or mid-action creates curiosity gap

## Output

For each hook angle, provide:
1. **Hook text** — what appears on screen or is spoken first
2. **Type** — which pattern it uses
3. **Why it works** — one sentence on the psychological mechanism
4. **Best platform** — which platform this hook fits best

Return as a numbered list. Label the strongest hook for first-second retention with [TOP PICK].
